A study in propagation

One loud voice fades. A ripple keeps moving.

Two ponds, the same network of people. On the left, a single node erupts with everything it has. On the right, one small touch — passed gently from neighbor to neighbor. Watch which one travels farther, and which one lasts.

  • Amplitude isn't impact. Loud is big but shallow — it washes over people and burns off. A quiet signal can land deep and stay.
  • Impact compounds. What genuinely moves your neighbor makes them move theirs. Small, real effects add up through feedback.
  • Recovery primes. A node that's been moved comes back more receptive than before — so the next ripple carries even further.
