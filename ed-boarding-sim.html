Admitted patients continue to occupy ED beds until inpatient placement (boarding). Watch occupancy, boarders, and the waiting queue evolve over time.

Reading the Output
Utilization (ρ): arrivals × mean LOS / beds. Steady-state queues form when ρ approaches 1. With defaults (7/hr × 6.4 hr / 50 = 0.896), the system is loaded but not saturated.
Boarding accumulation: admitted patients hold beds 6× longer than discharges. A small admit rate (12%) consumes a disproportionate share of capacity (~45% of bed-hours).
Queue tail: when occupancy hits 50, arrivals wait. The P95 envelope shows worst-case waits, which dominate left-without-being-seen and door-to-bed times.
Model Notes
Arrival process: homogeneous Poisson (constant λ) — no time-of-day or day-of-week variation. Real EDs peak afternoons / evenings.
LOS distribution: exponential with the entered mean. Real LOS is closer to log-normal; exponential gives a more conservative (heavier-tailed) view.
Admit decision: independent Bernoulli at arrival. Real disposition is correlated with acuity, age, and presenting complaint.
Boarding: modeled simply as a longer ED LOS for admitted patients. Inpatient bed availability is not modeled separately.
RNG: mulberry32 seeded PRNG so results are reproducible from the seed input.
⚠ Operations modeling tool. Outputs depend strongly on input assumptions (especially λ and admit-LOS). Calibrate against your department's actual arrival rates and LOS distributions before using for staffing or capacity decisions.
