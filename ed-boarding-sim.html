ED Boarding
Monte Carlo Bed Model

Coupled ED + inpatient discrete-event simulation. ED arrivals follow a Poisson process; after an ED workup period each visit either discharges or is admitted. Admitted patients board in the ED until an inpatient bed is available. IP discharges concentrate around a configurable hour (default noon) — each discharged bed enters a turnaround/cleaning interval before the next boarder can occupy it. Boarder time emerges from the system, not as a fixed LOS.

Reading the Output
Boarder time is endogenous: a boarder leaves the ED only when an IP bed clears + completes turnaround. Tighter IP capacity → longer boarder times → backed-up ED → waiting room → LWBS.
Noon discharge clustering: IP beds become available in a daily wave around the configured DC hour. Watch the IP grid turn yellow (turnaround) mid-day, then re-fill from boarders. Boarders pile up overnight and clear after noon.
IP utilization: admit-rate × arrivals × IP-LOS / IP-beds. With defaults (0.84/hr × 5.5d / 120 = 0.92), IP runs hot — small perturbations cause cascading delays.
Effective IP LOS: raw exponential mean is 5d, but rounding "ready" times forward to next noon adds ~12hr on average. Adjust IP-LOS-days down to ~4.5 if you want true 5d effective LOS.
Model Notes
Coupling: ED bed → ED workup → either discharge (88%) or boarder (12%) → IP bed → turnaround → IP bed reopens to next boarder. Boarders are FIFO; oldest boarder claims the next available IP bed.
Arrival process: homogeneous Poisson (constant λ) — no time-of-day or day-of-week variation. Real EDs peak afternoons / evenings.
ED workup: exponential with the entered mean (default 4 hr). Applies to all comers; admit decision happens at end of workup.
IP LOS: log-normal with target mean = IP-LOS-days × 24 hr and log-space σ (default 0.6 → CV ≈ 0.66, realistic for hospital data; CV = 1 corresponds to exponential). Each patient's "ready time" is then pushed forward to the next configured DC hour ± Gaussian noise (DC σ).
Turnaround (TAT): exponential mean 2 hr by default — captures cleaning, bed-board lag, and patient transport.
LWBS: waiting-room patients leave if their wait exceeds the threshold. Reasonable default is 4 hr.
Warm start: live IP unit pre-fills to ~80% with random remaining LOS; Monte Carlo discards the first 48 hr as warmup.
RNG: mulberry32 seeded PRNG so results are reproducible from the seed input.
⚠ Operations modeling tool. Outputs depend strongly on input assumptions (especially λ and admit-LOS). Calibrate against your department's actual arrival rates and LOS distributions before using for staffing or capacity decisions.
